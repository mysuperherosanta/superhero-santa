The story

The sleigh is packed. The sky is clear. And the sleigh Reindeer are gone.

Santa doesn't panic. He pulls on his boots, gathers his best elves and a very old map, and follows the hoofprints out across the snow, past the pines, under a sky full of stars.

Book One of the Superhero Santa series is a toddler bedtime reader for ages 1 to 3 — short lines, big pictures, made for the night before Christmas and every night in December.

The reason

Santa is the helper. Christmas has a hero.

In the Superhero Santa books, Santa is brave, kind and a little bit super — but he's not the point. Every adventure ends where Christmas begins: with a family, a star, and the birth of Jesus.
